## What qClip does

1. **Paste a link** — Twitch, YouTube, Kick, or a public `.mp4`
2. **Transcribe** — speech to text on your machine
3. **Find highlights** — software scores the best moments
4. **Render clips** — vertical videos ready for Shorts / TikTok
5. **Review and publish** — you choose what ships
