qClip

How it works — plain-English overview

qClip turns long streams and VODs into short, phone-ready clips. It finds the good moments, crops them for TikTok/Shorts/Reels, adds captions, and ranks what to post first — on your own computer.

In one line: Paste a link → wait → get ranked, captioned clips ready to post. No subscription. No watermark. You keep the files.

The journey (7 steps)

1

You start a job

Paste a URL or upload a file. The site doesn’t freeze — it starts background work and shows a progress bar.

2

Ingest

Download or save the source video once. Big files go into storage; the website never has to carry them.

3

Transcribe

Whisper listens to the whole video one time and writes a transcript with word timing — the script for captions.

4

Find highlights

Look for “wow” moments using loud audio, funny lines, motion, and chat spikes. Drop near-duplicates. Cut on word boundaries.

5

Rank clips

A local language model compares candidates and scores which ones feel more worth posting.

6

Make each clip

Track the person, crop for phones, stamp captions, optionally add memes/SFX, then export an MP4.

7

You review

Browse the ranked shortlist, keep the winners, and publish when you’re ready.

AI models & tools

  • faster-whisper

    Turns talking into timed text for captions and highlight clues.

  • YOLOv11 + ByteTrack

    Finds the subject and keeps them centered while cropping.

  • llama3.2 (Ollama)

    Compares clip ideas and ranks what should perform better.

  • MiniLM embedder

    Matches a clip’s joke/hook to the right meme asset.

  • librosa + ffmpeg

    Times sound effects; cuts and encodes the final video (NVENC on NVIDIA GPUs for speed).

Why it’s designed this way

Background jobs

A long VOD can take minutes. That work can’t live inside one button click.

GPU vs normal queue

Heavy vision/encode work doesn’t wait behind slow chat or downloads.

One full transcript

Listen once, reuse everywhere — faster than re-listening per clip.

Self-hosted

Your machine, your files, no watermark, no metered cloud clip tax.
